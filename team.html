Our team

The people who will actually run your operations.

ANA is not a directory of subcontractors. Our engagements are run by operators who have held these roles inside corporate offices and flagship business centres — people who have managed the floor, the vendors and the budget themselves.

Meet our Operations Head

Gomathi Chinnaswamy

Administration Head · Workplace Operations · Office & Facilities Management

An experienced Administration Head with over fifteen years managing corporate administration, workplace operations, facilities, employee services, procurement, vendor management, budgeting and office infrastructure.

She specialises in leading end-to-end administrative operations and building organised, efficient, safe and employee-friendly workplaces. She works closely with senior leadership and cross-functional teams to ensure workplace services and administrative processes support business objectives and hold a high standard of employee experience.

Her experience covers facility soft services, administrative budgets, procurement, vendor finalisation, employee relations, event management, access management, health and safety, cafeteria and transport operations, resource booking, MIS reporting and new-joiner onboarding.
