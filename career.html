Careers at ANA

Build a career running the operations everyone else relies on.

Administration is not overhead. It is the function that decides whether an office opens on time, whether a vendor delivers, and whether people can do their work. If that is the kind of responsibility you want, we should talk.

Apply now Email admin@anasupport.biz

Why ANA

A small practice, which means real scope early.

In a large company, administration is split into narrow roles and it can take years to see the whole picture. Here, you see all of it in your first few months.

Our people work across office setup and relocation, facilities, vendor management, procurement, travel and visa coordination and back-office administration — often on the same week. You will sit in client meetings, walk client floors, negotiate with vendors and own the follow-through, rather than processing one step of someone else’s workflow.

It is demanding work. Things break, vendors miss dates and somebody has to be accountable for fixing it. We are straightforward about that, because the people who do well here are the ones who want that responsibility.

What we offer

What you can expect working here.

Ownership from the start

You are given scope you are accountable for, not tasks you are handed. What you own is written down, and so is what good looks like.

Breadth across services

Facilities one week, vendor negotiation the next, a relocation the month after. You build range instead of repeating a single process.

Direct access to leadership

No layers between you and the people running the practice. You will be coached by operators who have done the job for fifteen years.

Client-facing exposure

You will represent ANA in front of client teams, landlords and vendors — the experience that makes an administrator into an operator.

Processes worth learning

Documentation, checklists, audit trails and service levels. You leave here knowing how a workplace should actually be run.

Growth as the practice grows

We are building. People who take responsibility early are the ones who end up leading engagements and, in time, teams.

We hire for ownership, not for job titles.

Qualifications tell us where you started. What we look for is whether you follow through when nobody is checking — because that is the whole job.

Who does well here

What we look for.

We have hired people from hospitality, front office, facilities, customer service and pure administration. The background matters less than these eight things.

  • You close the loop without being chased
  • You write clearly and keep records
  • You are comfortable in front of clients and vendors
  • You are organised under competing deadlines
  • You treat cost as if it were your own money
  • You escalate early instead of hiding a problem
  • You are willing to be on site, not only at a desk
  • You want to learn the whole operation, not one task

Our hiring process

Four steps, and we tell you where you stand at each one.

01

Application

Send your details and CV through the form below, or email them to admin@anasupport.biz. Every application is read, and you will hear back either way.

02

Introductory call

A short conversation about your experience, what you are looking for and what the role actually involves day to day. Usually within a week of applying.

03

Working discussion

A practical conversation about real situations — a vendor who has missed a deadline, a floor issue during business hours. We are interested in how you think, not in trick questions.

04

Offer and onboarding

Written role scope, compensation and start date. Onboarding pairs you with someone who has run the function, so you are not left to work it out alone.
