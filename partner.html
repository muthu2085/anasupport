What a partnership covers

Nine core services, delivered by one team.

Take all nine, or the three that are costing you the most time. Every engagement is scoped around the services you actually need managed.

01

Workplace Setup & Relocation

End-to-end coordination for new office setup, relocation, workplace planning, vendor coordination, infrastructure requirements and office handover.

02

Facilities & Workplace Management

Support for day-to-day workplace operations, maintenance coordination, housekeeping, security, office services, employee facilities and general workplace requirements.

03

Vendor & Procurement Management

Vendor sourcing, quotations, negotiations, purchase coordination, service management, documentation and follow-up to ensure smooth and timely delivery.

04

Domestic & International Travel

Complete business travel coordination — flights, hotels, ground transportation, itineraries, travel planning and expense coordination for employees and leadership teams.

05

Visa & Immigration Support

Coordination and administrative assistance for business and work-related visa processes, including documentation, appointment coordination, application tracking and travel readiness.

06

Foreign Exchange (Forex) Support

Assistance with foreign currency requirements for business travel, including coordination of forex arrangements, documentation and related administrative support.

07

Office & Back-Office Administration

Professional support for everyday administrative activities, documentation, coordination, records, scheduling, correspondence and operational follow-ups.

08

Employee & Workplace Support

Support for employee onboarding, workplace arrangements, engagement activities, internal events, administrative coordination and employee-related services.

09

Operational Coordination & Cost Control

Structured planning, tracking, budgeting support, documentation, vendor coordination and reporting to improve visibility and control over administrative operations.

Built around your business

Four ways a partnership is structured.

Every organisation has different operational requirements. Engagement models are set against your business size, location, workforce, travel needs and operational complexity.

Project-based

A defined scope with a start and an end — an office setup, a relocation, a fit-out, a single expansion. Quoted against a committed handover date.

Retained administration

A standing monthly engagement covering back-office administration, vendors, facilities and documentation, with consultant days committed to your floor.

Travel & mobility desk

Business travel, visa documentation and forex coordination run as a managed desk for employees and leadership, with the expense trail finance needs afterwards.

End-to-end operations

The full brief — workplace, vendors, travel, administration and cost control — owned by one partner and reported on a single cadence.
