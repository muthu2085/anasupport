About the practice

Fifteen years of workplace operations leadership, built role by role.

ANA Office & Admin Consultant is led by Alcina Agnes Aaron. The practice is grounded in operational experience across client service, workspace operations, office management and HR administration — not in a framework borrowed from a deck.

Background

Discipline first, credentials second.

She grew up in Chakradharpur, a railway town in Jharkhand — her father a railwayman, her mother a homemaker, her early years shaped by the discipline of the Railway School.

She began her career as a certified teacher at Tata Motor School, Jamshedpur, and quickly realised the classroom was not where she belonged. So she did something bigger: she moved to Chennai, a new state, with no map and no safety net, and started over.

What followed was over a decade of hands-on work across customer service, hospitality and operations — every one of those roles teaching her something the last one did not.
