Who we are

We take the complexity out of managing your workspace.

At ANA Office & Admin Consultant, our team handles workplace setup, relocation, vendor management, facilities coordination and back-office administration — all under one seamless, fully managed engagement.

What we do

The operational backbone that keeps your business running.

We enable your leadership team to focus on strategy and growth while we manage the operations that keep the business running smoothly.

Workplace setup, relocation, vendor management, facilities coordination and back-office administration are handled by one team, working to one plan and reporting on one cadence. Instead of coordinating several suppliers and absorbing the gaps between them, you hold a single working relationship with a team that knows your business.

Our people have run these functions inside corporate offices and flagship business centres, which is why our approach is built on operational experience rather than on a template.

Founder

Alcina Agnes Aaron

Founder · Office & Administrative Consulting

Over fifteen years in workplace operations, office management and HR administration across Chennai’s flagship business centres and corporate offices.

Alcina spent nearly eight years with The Executive Centre as Operations Manager, running three flagship Chennai locations — Tamarai Tech Park, Prestige Palladium and Olympia Teknos — with responsibility for workplace operations, client service, sales and community management across all three sites.

She then spent close to five years at Denodo Technologies as Office Manager and HR Administrator, taking ownership of corporate administration, vendor and facilities management, employee services and HR operations for the India office.

Earlier roles across customer service, consultative sales and guest relations — with Airtel DTH, Club Mahindra and Apollo Dental Spa — built the client communication and service standards that shape how ANA works today. She founded ANA to bring that combined experience to organisations that need their workplace and administration run properly.
