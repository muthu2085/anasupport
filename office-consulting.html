Office space consulting

Office space consulting for organisations that are outgrowing their premises.

Site search, commercial negotiation, interior fit-out, relocation and statutory compliance — managed as one engagement so the business is not just moved in, but set up to perform from day one.

Commercial case

Where an office consultant delivers measurable return.

Premises decisions are capital-intensive, slow to reverse and unforgiving of inexperience. These are the four areas where dedicated ownership changes the commercial outcome.

01

Cost efficiency and rate benchmarking

We tap into established vendor and real-estate networks to secure competitive rates — on space, infrastructure and services — cutting overhead that most companies absorb by default.

02

Schedule certainty

Office setup, relocation and expansion projects stall for one reason: nobody owns the timeline. We do — end to end — so delays do not become your problem.

03

Regulatory and lease assurance

Leases, compliance requirements and safety standards are easy to get wrong and expensive to get wrong twice. We navigate them so you do not have to learn the hard way.

04

Leadership capacity returned

Every hour your leadership spends managing workspace logistics is an hour not spent on revenue. We take the complexity off your plate — completely.

Engagement model

From requirement definition to handover.

01

Requirement & baseline

Headcount, growth plan, budget, location constraints and what the current setup is costing you.

02

Market survey & shortlist

Options across office types and micro-markets, with rates and trade-offs laid out plainly.

03

Fit-out & vendor delivery

Layout, furniture, IT, interiors and the vendor chain — sequenced against a real timeline.

04

Handover & ongoing support

Compliance closed out, documentation handed over, and ongoing support if you want it.

Nobody owns the timeline. That is why office projects slip.

We take the search, the fit-out, the vendors and the compliance as a single brief with a single owner — so the move-in date you were given is the move-in date you get.
